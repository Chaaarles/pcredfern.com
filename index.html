AlkisKalkis

AlkisKalkis is a silly product with serious plumbing: a Vinmonopolet comparison tool built around one noble metric, price per litre of pure alcohol.

The system crawls all 33k+ products every night using reverse-engineered Vinmonopolet app APIs, spreads requests to avoid throttling, and scans stock for every physical store plus the webshop.
